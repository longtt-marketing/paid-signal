Why this exists: the same gap in the author's own ads

The demo shop is fictional, so here is a real case: the author's own Meta ads sending traffic to Shopee affiliate links in Vietnam, 2 to 8 October 2026, the days the merchant platform's export covers, 33 ads. Clicks and spend come from Meta's API per ad; orders and commission from Shopee's official export, matched to each ad one-to-one by its link's Sub_id. Not PayPal, and not run through Paid Signal: it is why the author built it, shown where the author had real numbers. Motivation, not a result.
