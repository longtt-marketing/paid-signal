How matching works

A payment can match through an ad tag or name in the PayPal order, a Meta lead export with the same customer email, or an ad name in the invoice. Email matching stays in this browser.

Ad and PayPal files

1. Ads Manager export (CSV)
Export helpMeta: Ads Manager → Reports → Export table data → CSV, at ad level. Google Ads works too. Needs the ad name, amount spent and results (or leads, conversions).
2. PayPal activity download (CSV)
Export helpPayPal: Activity → Statements and reports → Activity download → All transactions, CSV, same dates. The ad comes from the order's custom field (Custom Number), a utm_content tag, or an item title equal to the ad name.
3. Meta lead form export (CSV, optional)
File detailsInclude created_time, ad_id, ad_name and email. UTF-8 CSV and UTF-16 tab-separated downloads are supported. Emails are matched locally and never shown.
