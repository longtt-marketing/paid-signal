Where to get these files, and how matching works

Meta: Ads Manager → Reports → Export table data → CSV, at ad level (Google Ads works too): the ad name, amount spent and results or leads. PayPal: Activity → Statements and reports → Activity download → All transactions, CSV, same dates. Meta lead form export: created_time, ad_id, ad_name and email; UTF-8 CSV or UTF-16 tab-separated.

A payment is matched to an ad by the tag in the PayPal order (custom field, a utm_content tag, or an item title equal to the ad name), then by the customer's email in the Meta lead export, then by the ad name in the invoice. Emails are matched locally and never shown. Everything runs in this page with the same money code the Worker uses (ledger.js); nothing is uploaded, and no AI reads your files.

The sample shop is small on purpose. The budget rule needs 3 paid orders before it moves a budget.
