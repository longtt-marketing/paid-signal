Audit your own ads against your own PayPal payments.

No login, no connection. Drop two files you can download today: your Ads Manager export and your PayPal activity download. Paid Signal shows which ads brought payments PayPal confirms, how much paid revenue no ad can claim, and what its budget rule would allow. The files never leave this browser.

Your two files

1. Ads Manager export (CSV)Meta: Ads Manager → Reports → Export table data → CSV, at ad level. Google Ads works too. Needs the ad name, amount spent and results (or leads, conversions).
2. PayPal activity download (CSV)PayPal: Activity → Statements and reports → Activity download → All transactions, CSV, same dates. The ad comes from the order's custom field (Custom Number), a utm_content tag, or an item title equal to the ad name.

Everything runs in this page with the same code the Worker uses (ledger.js); nothing is uploaded, and no AI reads your files. The sample files are the demo shop: a fictional ad export and its PayPal sandbox payments, read live.
