Ten ways money goes wrong.

Six run live in the PayPal sandbox, four in automated tests.

1 · Meta ad saleThe click rides in the order; the capture counts for that ad and goes to Meta onceLive
2 · Google ad saleThe gclid is kept the same way and becomes a Google offline conversion rowLive
3 · Invoice paid by a leadClaude drafts it, a person approves, the send agent sends it; the payment counts for the lead's adLive
4 · Pay pressed twiceA second payment from the same payer and ad is held, then refunded by the refund agentLive
5 · Full refundThe order stops counting; Google gets a retraction, the Meta event is held backLive
6 · Partial refundThe order still counts at $40; Google gets a restatementLive
7 · No ad clickPayPal confirms it, but no ad gets the credit and no platform is toldTest
8 · Paid long afterRevenue stays; no Meta event after 7 days, no Google row after 90Test
9 · Invented numberThe code gate rejects it, allows one repair, then flags itTest
10 · DisputeWhile open it is not a paid order; lost counts as refunded, won counts againTest
