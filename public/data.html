What data goes where

The shop and leads in this demo are fictional. PayPal payments and invoices are real sandbox records, and Meta receives test events only.

What Meta gets

For an eligible purchase from a Meta ad, Paid Signal sends a Purchase event to the demo's Meta test dataset. The event includes:

  • The buyer's email as a SHA-256 hash when it is available
  • The purchase value and currency
  • An event id made from the PayPal capture id
  • The Meta click id when one was recorded
  • The payment event time

These are test events. They do not count toward a real ad campaign.

What Google would get

For a Google ad click, Paid Signal prepares an offline conversion row with the Google click id, conversion name, payment time, value, currency, and order id. This demo only previews those rows for download. It does not send them to Google.

What is never sent to Meta

Paid Signal does not send card data, names, physical addresses, or a raw email address to Meta. The email used for a match is hashed before it enters the Meta event.

What the demo keeps

Cloudflare Workers KV stores payment and refund records, click ids, hashed email, event delivery status, invoice records, and approval records. An invoice record can include the lead's raw email so the sandbox invoice can be sent. Payment and invoice records have no automatic deletion date and remain until removed from KV. Rate limit counters expire after two days. Webhook receipt records expire after fourteen days.

For a real shop

Before enabling conversion sharing, explain what data goes to each ad platform and get the shop's required customer consent. Record consent, send events only when consent allows it, provide a way to refuse, and honor a later withdrawal. The demo does not collect or prove real customer consent.
