Does it make money? A simulation

Same weekly budget ($326.50), 8 weeks, replayed 2,000 times. Each ad's cost per lead and lead-to-paid rate come from the demo ad export and its PayPal results. One way moves budget toward the most leads per dollar, which is what ad platforms optimise for; the other moves it toward the most PayPal-confirmed paid orders per dollar, which is what Paid Signal shows. Both keep at least 10% on every ad. A simulation, not a measurement; it reruns on a real shop's numbers with node scripts/eval-budget-sim.mjs shop.csv.

$690profit over 8 weeks when budget follows paid orders69.5 paid orders on average.
$438profit when budget follows leads64.2 paid orders: the cheapest leads pay least often.
1,434 / 2,000runs where following paid orders brought more paid orders45 ties; the rest went the other way, because weekly results are noisy.
